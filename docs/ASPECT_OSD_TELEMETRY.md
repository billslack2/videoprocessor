# Aspect-ratio OSD telemetry

The VP Renderer OSD exposes exactly two aspect rows: **AR in use** and **Detected AR**. The HDMI input aspect is not a separate row. Startup uses the renderer's full input-raster geometry until safe picture evidence is available; the input aspect is not hard-coded to 16:9 or derived from the physical screen.

AR in use names the aspect of the final source rectangle after successful render/submission, before downstream NLS/anamorphic display mapping. It follows the selected temporal packet and presentation crop, including subtitle-related geometry. It never substitutes the latest detector candidate or an OSD-only held label for the geometry actually submitted. Existing source-pixel crop retention and safety decisions remain unchanged. Startup and detected geometry use the same normal label, such as 16:9 (HDTV). Held geometry and other presentation adjustments retain their qualifiers. Startup fallback remains an internal telemetry state, not a special OSD label.

Detected AR reports the current source-pixel physical measurement, independently of the accepted conservative crop or final presentation. It shows a numeric ratio with five decimal places and the measured source rectangle, with strong-edge or source-extent confidence. Familiar-ratio labels are reserved for AR in use; they never round or snap the detected rectangle. An evaluated but unresolved measurement reads Unknown (boundaries unresolved); a provisional nomination awaiting physical evaluation reads Unknown (awaiting physical measurement). It does not substitute a previously held rectangle, hide changes behind a UI deadband, or describe a completed-but-ambiguous measurement as pending. Raw measured/submitted values and source sequences remain exact in telemetry and logs.

No Netflix or 1.66:1 exclusion is introduced. VP-0222 rejects incomplete-axis pillarbox acquisition and withdraws an established side crop after three adjacent native observations show broad picture in both old side bars. Genuine 1.66:1 remains supported. The two rows distinguish live candidates from applied geometry; unavailable analysis views explicitly clear live detection. See VP-0222_PILLARBOX_AUTHORITY.md for the evidence and regression contract.

Snapshots are published without the render lock on the OSD thread. Source changes and renderer retirement clear incompatible state; output-only cache invalidation does not discard source facts. Shared host/renderer ABI is 34 and requires a matching runtime pair.
