That tolerance is max(2,width/480,height/270), eight source pixels at 4K. Horizontal coordinates always come from current evidence. New outward growth and larger changes apply immediately. Selection does not refresh history or alter its per-edge expiry; it grants no logical crop authority. The renderer invokes this only for current generic FIT, preserving the existing held-owner branch.
