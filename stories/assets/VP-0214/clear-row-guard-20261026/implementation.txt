Implementation
The final renderer recovery/admission path calls ProtectClearedSubtitleExpansion after both held detector-envelope and dense-FIT contributions have become the candidate geometry, and after uncertain-picture recovery. The veto only applies in Black/Reconstruction with a complete exact-source no-bright-ink result, current native BAR observation matching the prior submitted native base, no competing transition/full-raster/translation, and strict current full-row clearance covering every discarded row. The union also includes an already-submitted outward envelope. Identity, policy, continuity, raster and physical base must match. Any missing, stale, partial, colored or bright pixel proof declines protection.
The accepted decision retains the native base with SUBMITTED_SCOPE_RETENTION and goes through the existing admission and optional-side-fill path. It neither clears persistent raw/dense history nor changes glyph/cleanup authority. The label-dependent global recovery policy and skipped-dense-analysis timeout are intentionally unchanged.

Worker scope and cost
Advanced-mode requests opt into bounded full-row clearance on complete no-bright-ink scans. The request participates in worker cache identity. Classic/Off retain prior negative-scan behavior. All scanning is on the existing worker, capped at128 rows per side, with no render-thread fallback. This is a per-source advanced-mode request, not a renderer-history-dependent worker gate. The larger history provenance redesign was not implemented.
A standalone /O2 x64 P010 benchmark at3840x2160 measured100 warmed scans: average4.0578ms, p95 4.775ms, maximum5.615ms,983296 samples. This is local synthetic measurement, not a promise about other hardware/formats. Broader envelopes can exceed the cap and remain unprotected; log coverage shows why. Normal subtitle worker performance counters include this added work.

Existing VP submitted geometry remains the final output receipt.
Reasons distinguish missing current native observation, anchor mismatch, competing presentation, horizontal/non-expansion, submitted-envelope conflict, unavailable clearance, stale/mismatched context and uncovered rows. removed-rows-not-clear may mean a conflicting pixel or the bounded scan limit; sample count and interval distinguish coverage extent without claiming a complete black result.
