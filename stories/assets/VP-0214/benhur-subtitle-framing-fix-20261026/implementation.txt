Evidence and limits
The saved runtime logs show successful submitted source crops switching between
376-2056 and 388-1776 while trusted native picture remains 388-1772. Raw subtitle
sampling uses 384-1776. A positive bottom-bar cue can be rejected before pixel
comparison when the unrelated top edge becomes unavailable. Backing cleanup has
its own context rejection, so a retained glyph cue alone does not stabilize it.
Existing native captures precede the affected transitions. No claim is made that
the actual four-pixel source strip or the incident glyph pixels are unchanged.

Implementation
An exact current fit-ownership certificate travels separately from the native
crop owner through recovery, admission, selected output and successful submit.
Geometry, rather than the BAR_REFINEMENT/OUTWARD_FIT label, decides whether a new
outward presentation needs confirmation. Only actual successfully submitted
bounds can update the retained envelope; certificates cannot acquire native AR.

The subtitle guard can recheck a current positive bar-only cue when the opposite
edge disappears, including independently sampled/native edges that differ by
four pixels. Native glyph/backing samples are compared against a fixed baseline;
source identity, advancing source, format, grouping and epoch checks remain.
Such evidence is local glyph ownership only. Healthy current positives can keep
passing fresh verification beyond 250ms; detector misses retain the absolute
non-renewing cap. Paused display repeats do not renew it.

Backing cleanup requires the current cue-side raw bar plus current native body,
flanks and finite closure. Opposite-edge loss cannot veto that independent proof.
A held current-positive cue needs its exact bounded glyph certificate to satisfy
only cue ownership. That certificate supplies no backing or AR authority. Cached
outline coordinates survive only bounded uninspected context gaps, and current
pixels must re-prove cleanup each time. Cleanup is the neutral black intersection;
rounded live-picture corners and chroma-contaminated edge pixels are preserved.

Diagnostics now distinguish context failures from pixel comparison, report the
backing edge/context masks and candidate age, and show proposed versus resolved
presentation plus actual post-submit envelope changes and certificate identity.

Regression coverage
Production policy chain: original unqualified refinement bypass, certified
376-2056 submission followed by uncertainty, failed/replayed sources, selected
proof/frame/epoch/actual-geometry mismatches, genuine native expansion controls,
and real cold-caption builder through late qualification and successful commit.

Native subtitle chain: actual P010, P210 and decoded Dolby Vision sampling and
measurement, worker -> tracker -> padding/composition/fit/layout -> lip -> apply.
The combined case keeps glyphs wholly in the bar, backing-only picture intrusion,
and 384-1776 versus 388-1772 independent bounds. It runs over one second with the
opposite edge unavailable, then rejects late changed glyphs and an expired
negative cue before recovery. Controls cover missing/stale/nonlocal certificates,
raw-negative and incomplete/cached/held worker data, epoch/source/gap changes,
changed backing/glyphs, truncated ROI, cue-side tracking and lease expiry.
Backing cases also cover both bars, 2-16-pixel proved overlap plus fail-closed
one-row overlap, rounded corners, exact
chroma-edge trimming, closure/shadow limits and work-budget failures.

Baseline GPU test correction

assertion failed identically on clean beta53f7e234. Its unchanged-pixel probe was
inside the intentional six-pixel display overhang and two-pixel bar overlap. The
corrected test checks the overhang positively and probes unchanged pixels beyond
it. No shader or geometry behavior changes. The baseline DLL compiled and ran
the filtered test; its post-link runtime-record helper separately failed to find
Get-FileHash. That build is diagnostic evidence, not a deployment-qualified build.
