VIDEO PROCESSOR — STABLE FRAMING AND REAL ASPECT TRANSITIONS
Architecture proposal, 2026-10-31
Based on verified beta a591ad3b532771911e85f8ef026958279f2065f0 and the four-reviewer AR audit.
Status: designed and reviewed; not implemented, built, deployed, or performance-qualified.

OBJECTIVE
Retain established framing through uncertain measurements while promptly revealing
and accepting genuine source-picture changes. Native picture detection depends on
source pixels, never screen aspect, CIH, zoom, fill, NLS, or familiar ratio names.
A delay cannot make a wrong boundary correct. Both measurement authority and
presentation continuity need explicit, compatible rules.

1. SEPARATE FOUR FACTS

Accepted native picture
An accepted boundary record: immutable contract ID, source identity and format/
interpretation generation, exact rectangle, per-edge evidence, accepting source
sequence, and proof provenance. A later unknown measurement does not erase it.
Positive contrary evidence can mark affected edges contradicted while retaining
the old record as history; contradicted edges are no longer safe crop authority.

Current observation
Exact-frame, per-edge evidence with explicit provenance, sampling coverage and
completion: UNKNOWN, CONSISTENT_MATTE, or VISIBLE_OUTSIDE. Also carry independently
verified new boundary candidates. Visible content is typed as picture, caption/UI,
or unclassified. A missing measurement is not an unsafe measurement, and an unsafe
excluded band is not automatically proof of a new program aspect.

Last successfully submitted presentation
Actual source window and transforms that reached the display, linked to the
accepted native contract, selected source sequence, and presentation epoch.
Only successful rendering/submission commits this record. Fitted/refined output
must not be misreported as the native picture rectangle.

Temporary preservation envelope
The bounded source extent needed to preserve independently verified content outside
accepted geometry. It changes presentation, not detected native AR. It records why
it exists and the native/overlay evidence that owns it. It is not a permanent union
of every pixel ever seen: fresh evidence of disappearance or a qualified replacement
may retire it. Unknown evidence alone may not shrink a submitted envelope.

2. FIX THE CONTINUITY ANCHOR WITHOUT INVENTING NATIVE AUTHORITY

Accepted native geometry must survive even if its exact rectangle has never been
displayed. A first valid refinement, translation or FIT may submit a different
rectangle. The selected packet carries two independent records:
  - the accepted native geometry contract that authorizes the picture;
  - the exact authorized presentation derived from it.
On successful submission, atomically commit their association and the actual
presentation. Do not require an earlier exact-native submission. Do not derive
native authority from a FIT rectangle or a matching aspect ratio.

This replaces the circular prerequisite in CommitSubmittedNativeScopeAnchor:
previous anchor -> qualified FIT envelope -> retained anchor. It fixes the Prime
sequence where valid refinement/FIT reached the display with anchor unavailable.
Failed, cancelled, superseded or wrong-source submissions cannot advance either
presentation continuity or its ordering watermark.

On a profile/viewport change, retain compatible accepted native geometry, invalidate
profile-specific placement, and recompute presentation. Do not blindly carry old
screen transforms across epochs. An actual incompatible source/format change clears
old native authority and starts with that input's full native raster.

3. ONE NATIVE CANDIDATE VERIFIER

Coarse scans nominate rectangles; they do not grant crop authority themselves.
Route every candidate through one verifier before BAR_CROP_TRUSTED, including
ordinary bright-edge candidates, recovery candidates, remembered returns, and
horizontal/vertical/windowbox combinations. Cached proof is reusable only for its
exact source, interpretation, bounds, inspected bands and completion identity.

Keep these quantities separate:
  - declared-range nominal black;
  - coarse near-black classification threshold;
  - actually measured matte Y/U/V reference and noise/dispersion;
  - picture-versus-matte contrast at each measured boundary.
Code-12 matte remains measured as12 even if a classifier uses reference0/cutoff24.
A flat gray region alone is not enough to establish a bar.

For each band the candidate would discard, verify consistency with the qualified
measured matte reference, inspect independent boundary transitions, and check
spatial/depth coverage. A broad code80 picture band is not equivalent to code64
matte merely because both lie below cutoff88. That must disqualify the synthetic
72..288 crop before it receives trusted authority.

Verifier outcomes:
  VERIFIED: coherent boundaries and qualified excluded bands for these exact bounds.
  CONTRADICTED: reliable source evidence establishes visible content being excluded.
  UNKNOWN: incomplete scan, weak/ambiguous edge, noise, cancellation, or exhausted budget.
Unknown and contradicted inward candidates both fail acquisition; neither grants
full-raster authority. Contradiction includes observed coordinates and provenance.

Recover dim/dim as well as bright/dim boundaries from qualified outer references;
do not require a strong already-trusted opposing edge. Measure edges independently.
Symmetry can corroborate evidence but cannot invent, center, or reject an otherwise
verified asymmetric picture. Chroma-only authority and noise-relative thresholds
require calibration against negative controls before release.

4. A SINGLE TRANSITION DECISION, THEN PRESENTATION DERIVATION

Introduce a small transition coordinator around the existing model and proof
functions. It emits an explicit decision and reason. Downstream subtitle/FIT/NLS
code may transform an authorized presentation but cannot independently reset native
geometry because it lacks its own confidence or owner.

Decision sequence:
  a. Validate source/format and evidence identities.
  b. If no accepted native geometry, use native raster until verified acquisition.
  c. Verify candidates and collect exact-current per-edge contrary evidence.
  d. UNKNOWN/provisional/unavailable: retain accepted native geometry and compatible
     submitted presentation, unless separate positive evidence proves it unsafe.
  e. Strong outside content: form a bounded preservation extent from verified pixels.
     Do not manufacture a replacement AR from that extent.
  f. Confirm a coherent new native boundary using the appropriate source-history rule.
  g. Derive subtitle/FIT/fill/NLS presentation from that decision and current profile.
  h. Revalidate selected packet/context and commit actual presentation on success.

Make the reason explicit: STARTUP, HOLD_UNCERTAIN, PRESERVE_VISIBLE_CONTENT,
ACCEPT_NATIVE_CHANGE, or RESET_INCOMPATIBLE_SOURCE. Full input may also be the
correct result of confirmed full-raster picture or a preservation extent that
actually reaches the raster. It is never a substitute for UNKNOWN.

Expansion and contraction are deliberately asymmetric:
- Expansion: qualified current visibility can promptly preserve exposed content
  before the new aspect is committed. A low-confidence rectangle or isolated noise
  is insufficient. Broad picture proof can confirm an AR transition without waiting
  for a caption/FIT owner.
- Contraction: removing more pixels requires a verified new boundary and qualified
  evidence over every newly excluded band. Darkness alone is not such proof.
- Captions/UI: preserve verified occupied pixels through the presentation path;
  do not count their presence as native picture-boundary votes.
- One-sided/asymmetric changes: admit each independently proved edge; an opposite
  unknown edge cannot erase a valid preservation witness. Native replacement still
  requires a coherent rectangle certificate from a compatible observation.
- Genuine windowbox: provide an explicit verified acquisition/transition route.
  A composition-preservation setting may affect presentation but must not suppress
  accurate source measurement or silently freeze the detector's native aspect.

Keep existing three adjacent-source outward confirmations and four-observation
acquisition as initial compatibility settings, not universal truths. Do not add a
blanket larger delay. Matching evidence may satisfy multiple proof predicates;
avoid restarting a second dwell after the same evidence already qualifies a change.
Record distinct qualifying source samples and actual sampling cadence. Repeated
presentation of one source identity adds zero votes. A paused image delivered as
new capture samples can establish temporal stability, but repetition cannot repair
missing spatial boundary evidence. Late future/queued proof cannot apply before
its selected source frame or replace a newer successful decision.

5. BOUNDED WORK AND INCOMPLETE EVIDENCE

Share native measurements among acquisition, retention and transition checks using
exact-context cache keys. Check cheap contradictions early; apply dense boundary/
excluded-band inspection only where needed. Cover both axes and all scan phases.
Enforce a combined work budget; cancellation yields UNKNOWN, never partial authority.
A sparse grid cannot promise preservation of every isolated pixel. Prototype dense
row/tile extrema or efficient complete-band readers if sparse coverage fails caption
or thin-content acceptance. Measure worst-case CPU/work latency before selecting
production thresholds or budgets; no performance claim is made by this design.

6. IMPLEMENT IN SMALL, REVIEWABLE STEPS

A. Restore and strengthen the three saved regression probes. Add an end-to-end
   selected-submission harness recording native contract, candidate, final crop,
   successful submission and OSD. Include first-refinement, first-FIT and failed
   submission controls. Preserve existing genuine expansion negatives/positives.
B. Introduce explicit native/submitted records and repair initial continuity.
   Convert legacy booleans at an adapter boundary, then remove contradictory
   absence-of-proof-to-full-raster paths once the final-chain tests cover them.
C. Separate measured matte statistics from classifier references. Route all new
   crop acceptance through common verification and fix symmetric dark overcrop.
D. Integrate typed outside-picture/overlay witnesses with bounded preservation.
   Retire obsolete caption-owner prerequisites only for qualified native witnesses.
   Add actual native captured fixtures before broadening ambiguous raw-visible cases.
E. Consolidate transition ownership and eliminate duplicate confirmation delays.
   Keep generation/sequence/queue/epoch validation at admission AND submission.

These are local boundaries in ActivePictureEvidence, ActivePictureTransitionModel,
AlphaSourceCropPolicy, and LibplaceboVideoRenderer; avoid a wholesale renderer rewrite.
Do not ship two active authorities whose disagreement silently selects full raster.

7. ACCEPTANCE MATRIX

- Prime component sequence: accepted256..1904, submitted refinement262..1896,
  then provisional254..1766. Zero unsupported full-raster submissions. Accepted
  native geometry remains available; actual presentation continuity stays separate.
- Symmetric low-contrast source: true32..328, bright core72..288. Never accept the
  inner crop. Recover measured boundaries with explicit outward guard or remain
  unknown while preserving existing valid geometry.
- Full/Limited and black offsets: floors0,8,12,20,24,40,64; equivalent signal
  offsets, native formats, stride padding, code noise and chroma controls.
- Scope -> IMAX -> Scope, both bright and dim, cuts and gradual changes: preserve
  new visible picture promptly; publish measured boundaries after qualifying proof;
  no indefinite owner wait; do not contract before discarded-band validation.
- Prolonged dark/provisional/unavailable source and exhausted analysis budget:
  zero speculative native changes and zero uncertainty-driven raster resets.
- Genuine 16:9, 21:9, scope, pillarbox, nonstandard, asymmetric and windowbox:
  independent measured edges; no forced ratio or screen-dependent detection.
- Subtitles, tickers, menus, logos, fade, gradients, noisy/raised/tinted matte:
  preserve meaningful occupied pixels without granting false native format votes.
- Same source across presentation profiles: identical detector evidence/geometry;
  only intended downstream presentation transforms differ.
- Repeats, gaps, stale worker completions, real source changes, profile changes,
  render failure and unselected queued output: no stale proof promotion or renewal.
- Existing 97 Eternals frames: retain asserted0,60-3840,2096 acquisition and old
  Scope release. Prime pixel-level accuracy still needs the offending native frame.

Record boundary error (pixels), known clipped picture/caption pixels, false-crop
and unsupported-full-raster frame counts, native aspect error, qualifying sample
count, and source-to-submission latency. Check final submitted geometry and OSD,
not merely intermediate policy helper results. Preserve the existing 993 tests
unless a test encodes the rejected behavior; replace such expectations explicitly
with both uncertainty-hold and true-transition controls, not blanket deletion.

VALIDATION STATUS
The audit's unchanged-beta baseline passed993 existing tests. Its three new probes
failed and establish the defects being addressed. Those results are NOT validation
of this unimplemented architecture. Numeric dim-edge/noise thresholds, one-sided
admission, exhaustive coverage and runtime cost require prototype measurements.
