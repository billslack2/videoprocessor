Meaning of whole-picture fallback
A brief repeat of an already completed picture, including its subtitle and replacement background. It is not an aspect-ratio/full-raster fallback. It remains bounded to two missing source intervals (about 83 ms at 24 fps); inserted outputs cannot renew it.

Shared processing rule
AR, bar-content inspection, scene detection and subtitle acquisition use the owned source frame. Extra display outputs reuse prepared pictures and frozen source geometry. Live pose, target checks, OSD and render/HDR publication remain per output. In ordinary progressive Smooth Motion, libplacebo blends complete prepared pictures. Blended pixels do not create a fresh subtitle-absence decision.

Narrow fixes in this audit
- Exact repeats of a prepared discontinuity source reuse that successful preparation instead of rerunning inspection/resetting temporal state.
- A late same-source complete cue must update owned GPU pixels and source/mapping metadata together; failed upgrades retire the transaction consistently.
- AR-only retries preserve an already complete subtitle packet without another reconstruction pass.
