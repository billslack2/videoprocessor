Source: billslack2/videoprocessor v1.4.02-beta, 81ac960fbd62619374fba1c16630b8feac364d59.
No production source edits or deployment. Investigation follows first finding in five-luna-review-20261025.

1. Confirmed conditional state-machine defect: qualified frame projection suppresses persistent presentation evidence only in a local view.
SubtitleAspectRetention.h:550-565 copies raw history, then empties only result.envelope for qualified current source. LibplaceboVideoRenderer.cpp:15190 makes suppression depend on qualified proof or the unknown hold. On a complete negative scan, EvaluateQualifiedSubtitleUnknownHold (SubtitleAspectRetention.h:640) rejects the unknown lease. The next unqualified frame therefore exposes old history again. UpdatePresentationEnvelopeExtents (AlphaSourceCropPolicy.cpp:1991) retains old per-edge extents within their hold, even when the current bounds are the native base. SelectPresentationEnvelopeBounds (:2059) chooses that history before current bounds.
This is conditional on compatible, unexpired state and an active FIT route. It is not evidence that every cue gap must flash.

2. There are TWO retained inputs: generic envelope and dense vertical FIT.
Renderer :15249 enables stored vertical action on matching generation/base and no current suppression, without requiring a fresh dense scan. :15380 accepts stored FIT; :15528 routes the held detector top/bottom; :15538 also accepts retained dense extents; :15595 composes them with padding. Incident frame2871 explicitly has dense_scan=skipped, dense_evaluated=0, vertical_action=fit, envelope_state=held, generic_fit_held=0. Detector 270..1948 and dense bottom1916 plus44 compose exactly 270..1960. Thus clearing only raw scopePresentationHistory would not remove the independent dense contribution.

3. Confirmation precedence violates the stated native-base-retention contract / produces misleading ownership.
AlphaSourceCropPolicy.h Input comments promise retaining the trusted base during translation confirmation. AlphaSourceCropPolicy.cpp:6458 admits same-generation/base pending confirmation. :6522 onward applies outward geometry first; :6645 onward assigns TRANSLATION_CONFIRMATION and says trusted crop retained even with outwardExpanded=true. The final advanced-mode SubtitlePresentationBounds helper only undoes translation, not expansion; its noCaptionConfirmed argument is ignored. A pending translation does not protect scale here.

4. Related timing loophole, separately reproduced, not established as the incident's trigger.
AlphaSourceCropPolicy.cpp:876 IsVerticalBarPresentationActiveForFrame returns true after ordinary hold expiry when current dense analysis was skipped, current bar authority exists and generation matches. A FIT last seen at1000ms remains active at10000ms with hold2000ms in that branch. Current-analysis and generation-change controls return false. Consequently the nominal hold is not a hard upper bound on stored dense presentation while scans are skipped. Incident onset had skipped scans, but logs cited here do not establish a nine-second-old state; do not claim that duration occurred live.
