DXGI timing semantic correction
A proposed PresentRefreshCount<=SyncRefreshCount validation was rejected after primary-document review. Microsoft documents SyncRefreshCount/QPC at submission-related sampling, while PresentRefreshCount identifies actual display; a queued presentation can be displayed later. Equal deltas are likewise not required. The new QueuedLateScanoutWithEarlierSyncClockRemainsCountable test correctly covers sync10-to11 versus actual20-to23. The observer must validate each timeline's monotonic/cadence contract without conflating those instants.
