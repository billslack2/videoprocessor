Hold semantics
RendererProfileConfig::ResolveOffZoomHoldMs parses Off's independent timer
with default 2000 ms. Off and Classic have separate settings/behavior.
Hold is release hysteresis after content disappears, not a substitute for
stable final fit ownership while content is still visible.
A settings log printed during live profile switching can describe the prior
settings; its subtitle_fit value alone does not prove Off enabled Classic.
That initial interpretation was corrected during the original investigation.

Strong working hypothesis (not yet a proven minimal root cause)
Measured Off fit and retained successful-output envelope can take turns
controlling final geometry. Timer retention upstream does not prevent a
later uncertain-picture decision from restoring the prior 2068 envelope,
then a later confirmed current fit reducing it to 1974. Source/output lag
may feed that old rectangle back into subsequent decisions. The evidence
localizes the bug after measurement; it does not yet prove exactly which
confirmation flag or selected-output state update first becomes inconsistent.
