# A spectro (i1Pro): asks for a white-tile calibration first, then reads.
OUT Setting up the instrument
OUT
OUT Instrument needs a calibration before continuing
OUT Place instrument on white calibration reference,
OUT  and then hit any key to continue,
PROMPT  or hit Esc or Q to abort:
KEY
SLEEP 100
OUT
OUT Calibration complete
OUT
OUT Place instrument on spot to be measured,
OUT and hit [A-Z] to read white and setup FWA compensation (keyed to letter)
OUT [a-z] or space to read, 'r' to set reference, 's' to save spectrum,
PROMPT 'h' to toggle high res., 'k' to do a calibration
KEY
READ 48.10 50.00 54.20
OUT
OUT Place instrument on spot to be measured,
PROMPT [a-z] or space to read:
KEY
EXIT 0
